01 Profile

Technology across the whole stack of a business

Not a specialist in one layer — the infrastructure underneath, the ERP that runs on it, the security around it, and the applications people actually touch.

I'm an IT professional based in Baghdad who enjoys helping people feel more confident with technology — whether that's fixing a problem, explaining something in plain language, or showing a better way to work.
